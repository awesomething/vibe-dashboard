# Off the Hook — landing page

## Scope
One Persuade landing page for Off the Hook Barber Shop in Atlanta.

## Visitor and outcome
People around downtown Atlanta looking for a haircut should quickly recognize the shop, trust it through the supplied reviews, and call, pursue online booking, or get directions.

## Evidence and constraints
Use the supplied 4.8 rating from 289 reviews and the three supplied review excerpts verbatim. Use the provided phone, address, and Maps link. Do not invent services, prices, hours, staff identities, booking availability, or claims. The online booking experience is frontend-only; clearly disclose that it is not connected and do not claim a request was sent.

## Direction
The user chose the Peters Street atlas: an editorial city-map / appointment folio in deep maritime teal, pale stone, and persimmon. A large barber portrait and oversized type lead to verified reviews, the booking interface, and directions. The memorable moment is a street-map line that turns the Peters Street address into a path toward the chair and the primary action. The honest risk is that it may read more like a design-led city guide than a neighborhood barbershop.

## Added content
The shop-notes section surfaces themes directly from the supplied review excerpts. The lookbook uses illustrative barbering reference photography and must not be presented as verified Off the Hook client work; replace it with actual shop images when available.

THESIS: Make the route to a trusted cut feel as clear as finding a familiar Atlanta corner; refuse the generic black-and-gold barbershop template.

OWN-WORLD: City-atlas annotations, editorial folio typography, deep maritime teal, pale stone, persimmon wayfinding marks, and a decisive real barber photograph.

STORY: Recognize Off the Hook, see the 4.8 / 289 review proof and exact customer excerpts, then choose a call, frontend-only booking inquiry, or map directions.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact folio masthead sits above an oversized two-part headline and primary booking action; a large barber photograph is paired with a custom Peters Street map treatment and a visible review signal. At mobile widths, stack headline, action, image, then location cue without shrinking the headline into a banner.
